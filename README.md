## 📌 Project Overview

Predicting electrical energy output is crucial for optimizing power plant operations and grid stability. This project uses the UCI Combined Cycle Power Plant dataset to train and evaluate predictive models based on ambient variables collected over a 6-year period (2006–2011).<br>
<br>
A combined cycle power plant (CCPP) is composed of gas turbines (GT), steam turbines (ST) and heat recovery steam generators. In a CCPP, the electricity is generated by gas and steam turbines, which are combined in one cycle, and is transferred from one turbine to another. While the Vacuum is collected from and has effect on the Steam Turbine, the other three of the ambient variables affect the GT performance.

## 📊 Dataset Information

###  Dataset Link

[https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/294/combined+cycle+power+plant](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/294/combined+cycle+power+plant)

The dataset consists of **9,568 data points** collected from a Combined Cycle Power Plant operating at full load.<br>
<br>
% of the data is used for training and % is used for testing.<br>
<br>
Predictions are made for the net hourly electrical energy output (PE) of the plant using the other variables as features.

### Features (Predictors)

| Feature | Description | Unit |
|---------|-------------|------|
| AT | Ambient Temperature | °C |
| V | Exhaust Vacuum | cm Hg |
| AP | Ambient Pressure | mbar |
| RH | Relative Humidity | % |


### Target (Response)
| Target | Description | Unit |
|---------|-------------|------|
| PE | Net Hourly Electrical Energy Output | MW |
